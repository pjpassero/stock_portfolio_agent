# Correlation Overview

Correlation measures the strength and direction of a relationship between two variables, in this 
case the two variables are stocks, stock a and stock b. 

The correlation value is normalized on a scale from -1.0 to 1.0. 

A value greater than 0 means that both varaibles increase or decrease together.

A value less than 0 means that when one variable increases, the other decreases.

A correlation of 0 means that there is no linear relationship between the variables.

Correlation shows a relationship but it doesn't prove that one variable causes the other. There is no 
causation. 


For stocks, the correlation is computed on returns rather than prices.
